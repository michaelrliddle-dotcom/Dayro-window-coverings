Common questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How much do new window coverings cost?

It depends on the product, the size of the openings, and how many windows. We measure first and give you a written price per window before anything is ordered, so you can add or drop rooms and see exactly what it changes.

Do you really do free estimates and free installation?

Yes. The in-home consultation, the measuring, and the installation are all included. You pay for the product.

How long does an order take?

Most orders are measured, built, and installed in about two weeks. Specialty shutters and large motorized orders can run longer, and we'll tell you the timeline before you commit.

Which covering is right for my windows?

It comes down to light, privacy, and the room. Solar screens keep a view, blackout roller shades suit bedrooms, honeycombs help with heat and energy bills, verticals handle sliders, and shutters add long-term value. We bring samples and walk through it with you.

Can you motorize shades in an existing home?

Yes. Battery-powered motors need no wiring and work in almost any existing window. Hardwired systems are an option on remodels and new construction.

What areas do you serve?

Los Angeles, the South Bay, and Orange County — including Torrance, Carson, Long Beach, Anaheim, Irvine, and the surrounding cities.
